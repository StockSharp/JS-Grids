@stocksharp/grids is the browser data grid behind StockSharp's web applications — one column declaration drives the header, the body, the sort and the exported sheet. This page runs the published bundle over 25 sample orders: sort by clicking a header, pick and reorder columns, keep pinned summary rows outside the sort, cap what is painted, and download a real .xlsx.

What is live here
  • Click a header — asc → desc → back to the table default (id desc).
  • Columns… — ColumnSettings over this table: tick to show, arrows to reorder.
  • WORKING / ALL ORDERS — pinned rows: outside the sort, outside the sheet.
  • Cancel — a real <button> returned by render(), with its own listener.
  • Click a row — selection through bindRow + rowClass.
  • Live prices — mutate the held array, render(), flash the changed cell via cellElement().
  • renderLimit — caps the paint, never the sheet.
